FEATURE 01

Resume & Experience Scanner

Precision feature extraction with complete student review control.

How It Works:

  • Upload PDF: Ingest any standard text resume or paste raw bio text.
  • PyMuPDF Engine: Extracts technical skills, frameworks, and education.
  • Alias Normalizer: Matches variations (e.g., "JS" = "JavaScript").
  • 100% Privacy: Session-isolated storage with zero external sharing.

The Human Review Layer:

  • Zero Hallucinations: Never fabricates skills you didn't possess.
  • 1-Tap Verification: Add, edit, or remove skills with one click.
  • Transparent Evidence: See exact sentences from your resume.
  • Confidence: Your learning roadmap is grounded in reality.
